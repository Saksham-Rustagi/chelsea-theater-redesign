Other ways to give

  • Employer matching gifts: many companies match employee donations.
  • Donor-advised funds: give through your DAF provider.
  • Tribute gifts: honor or remember someone special.
  • IRA rollovers: a tax-efficient option for donors 70½ and older.
  • Stock transfers: avoid capital gains on appreciated securities.
