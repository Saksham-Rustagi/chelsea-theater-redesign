Accessibility

Wheelchair-accessible seating in every auditorium. Free assistive listening devices are available at the concession stand, and closed-caption devices and audio-description headsets are available for most shows. Open-caption screenings are marked on the showtimes.

Good to know

  • Where do I park? In the free lot right next to the theater.
  • Do members get discounts? Yes, on every regular show. Become a member.
  • Can I rent a theater? Yes, for private screenings, parties, and events. Rentals.
