Our story

A community cinema, built by Chapel Hill

The Chelsea presents films that entertain, inspire, and engage, and it brings our community together around them.

Who we are

The Chelsea is Orange County’s only nonprofit arthouse theater. For more than thirty years it has shown the independent, international, and classic films you won’t find at the multiplex.

As a community-supported nonprofit, the Chelsea has added repertory series, guest speakers, and partnerships with UNC and local groups, and it offers open-caption screenings every week.
