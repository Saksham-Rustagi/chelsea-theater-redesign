Our story

For more than thirty years, the Chelsea has shown the films that don’t play anywhere else in town, and brought Chapel Hill together to watch them.

Who we are

The Chelsea is Orange County’s only nonprofit arthouse theater. Its mission is to present films that entertain, inspire, and engage.

Today it shows more than 500 films a year to more than 50,000 visitors, with repertory series, guest speakers, open-caption screenings, and partnerships with UNC and local groups.
